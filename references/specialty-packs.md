# Specialty routing packs

> **What this file is — and deliberately is not.**
> It contains **routing information**: which bodies are authoritative, what must never be
> missed, which criteria sets exist *by name*, and which entities are classically mistaken for
> each other. It contains **no clinical facts** — no doses, no thresholds, no cut-offs.
>
> That is a design decision, not an omission. Clinical values go stale and are exactly where
> confident errors live (`LOOK UP, DON'T GUESS`). Named criteria are stable; their contents are
> not. **Use a criteria set's name to go and retrieve it — never to recite it from memory.**
>
> The "must-not-miss" lists are **screening prompts for the differential**, not differentials
> themselves, and they are not exhaustive. Absence from a list means nothing.

**How to use:** at P1, identify the involved system(s) and load the matching pack(s). At P2,
route guideline searches to the listed bodies first. At P2c/P4, run the must-not-miss list and
the mimic pairs against the case. At P7, confirm the list was run.

---

## Cardiology
- **Bodies:** ESC · ACC/AHA · HFSA · EHRA · NICE
- **Must-not-miss:** acute coronary syndrome · aortic dissection · pulmonary embolism · tamponade · critical aortic stenosis · infective endocarditis · myocarditis · long-QT/channelopathy in young syncope
- **Named criteria/scores:** Duke (endocarditis) · Wells PE · PERC · HEART · GRACE · TIMI · CHA₂DS₂-VASc · HAS-BLED · NYHA · ACC/AHA HF stages · Sgarbossa
- **Classic mimics:** dissection ↔ ACS · PE ↔ anxiety/pleurisy · myocarditis ↔ ACS (young) · amyloid ↔ HFpEF/"hypertensive" LVH · POTS ↔ panic
- **Hinge variables:** LVEF · troponin trajectory (not a single value) · natriuretic peptides · ischaemia assessment

## Endocrinology & metabolic
- **Bodies:** ADA/EASD · Endocrine Society · ATA · AACE · ESE
- **Must-not-miss:** adrenal crisis · myxoedema coma · thyroid storm · DKA/HHS · pituitary apoplexy · hypercalcaemic crisis · phaeochromocytoma · insulinoma
- **Named criteria:** ADA diagnostic criteria (DM) · Endocrine Society adrenal insufficiency · Bethesda (thyroid cytology) · Rotterdam (PCOS) · Endocrine Society hypoglycaemia (Whipple's triad)
- **Classic mimics:** adrenal insufficiency ↔ depression/CFS · phaeo ↔ panic disorder · hyperthyroidism ↔ anxiety · hypercalcaemia ↔ "functional" fatigue · hypothyroidism ↔ dementia
- **Note:** endocrine disease is the classic **episodic + provoked** presenter — apply the P1 episodic intake and the P2c trigger heuristic. Dynamic/confirmatory testing, and its **timing**, is usually the hinge.

## Nephrology
- **Bodies:** KDIGO · ERA-EDTA · NICE · national societies
- **Must-not-miss:** rapidly progressive glomerulonephritis · obstructive uropathy · hyperkalaemia · renal artery stenosis/scleroderma renal crisis · TTP/HUS · myeloma kidney · contrast/drug nephrotoxicity
- **Named criteria:** KDIGO AKI and CKD staging · CKD-EPI · ISN/RPS (lupus nephritis) · Oxford MEST-C (IgA)
- **Classic mimics:** PSGN ↔ lupus nephritis · myeloma ↔ "age-related" CKD · pre-renal ↔ ATN
- **Cross-check:** always reconcile eGFR with drug dosing (P2 §2.4)

## Rheumatology & immunology
- **Bodies:** ACR · EULAR · ESID/ICON (immunodeficiency) · BSR
- **Must-not-miss:** giant cell arteritis · septic arthritis · scleroderma renal crisis · macrophage activation syndrome · catastrophic APS · ANCA vasculitis with pulmonary–renal syndrome · myositis with ILD
- **Named criteria:** ACR/EULAR (RA, SLE, Sjögren, ANCA vasculitis, systemic sclerosis, giant cell arteritis) · Sapporo/Sydney (APS) · ASAS (spondyloarthritis) · ESID/ICON (CVID and other PIDs) · CASPAR (psoriatic arthritis)
- **Classic mimics:** VEXAS ↔ seronegative inflammatory disease + macrocytic anaemia · Felty ↔ infection · sarcoid ↔ almost anything (incl. GLILD) · IgG4-related disease ↔ malignancy · fibromyalgia label masking inflammatory disease
- **Occupational link:** silica → scleroderma/RA/SLE. Run the exposure history (P1) in every unexplained autoimmune presentation.

## Neurology
- **Bodies:** AAN · EAN · EFNS · MDS (movement disorders) · IHS (headache) · NICE
- **Must-not-miss:** stroke in the thrombolysis window · subarachnoid haemorrhage · bacterial meningitis/encephalitis · cord compression · Guillain-Barré with respiratory involvement · temporal arteritis with visual loss · Wernicke encephalopathy · status epilepticus · autoimmune encephalitis
- **Named criteria:** McDonald (MS) · ICHD (headache) · MDS (Parkinson) · Brighton (GBS) · Graus (autoimmune encephalitis) · NINDS-AIREN, MDS-PSP
- **Classic mimics:** functional neurological disorder ↔ organic (in both directions) · B12/copper deficiency ↔ MS · autoimmune encephalitis ↔ primary psychiatric presentation · migraine aura ↔ TIA
- **Note:** deficit localisation and time-from-onset are hinges before any imaging discussion.

## Oncology & haematology
- **Bodies:** ASCO · ESMO · NCCN · ASH · EHA
- **Must-not-miss:** febrile neutropenia · spinal cord compression · SVC syndrome · tumour lysis · hypercalcaemia of malignancy · leukostasis · TTP · acute promyelocytic leukaemia
- **Named criteria:** TNM/AJCC · RECIST · ECOG/Karnofsky · Lugano (lymphoma) · IMWG (myeloma) · WHO/ICC haematologic classification · IPSS-R · PLASMIC (TTP)
- **Classic mimics:** lymphoma ↔ sarcoid/TB · myeloma ↔ osteoporosis/CKD · paraneoplastic syndrome ↔ primary neurologic or rheumatologic disease
- **Hinges:** histology · stage · biomarkers/receptors · performance status. **Never stage or recommend without histology.**

## Infectious disease
- **Bodies:** IDSA · ESCMID · WHO · PAHO · national/regional health authorities
- **Must-not-miss:** sepsis/septic shock · necrotising fasciitis · meningitis · endocarditis · malaria (in the returning traveller) · TB · HIV seroconversion · neutropenic fever
- **Named criteria:** Sepsis-3/qSOFA · Duke · CURB-65 · Centor/McIsaac · IDSA syndrome-specific guidance
- **Classic mimics:** TB ↔ malignancy/sarcoid · endocarditis ↔ vasculitis · rickettsial/arboviral illness ↔ "viral syndrome" · Still's disease ↔ occult infection
- **Regional note (LatAm):** always consider Chagas, dengue/chikungunya/Zika, coccidioidomycosis, histoplasmosis, leptospirosis, brucellosis, cysticercosis by **geography and exposure**, not by presentation alone.

## Pulmonology
- **Bodies:** ATS/ERS · GOLD (COPD) · GINA (asthma) · CHEST
- **Must-not-miss:** PE · tension pneumothorax · massive haemoptysis · impending respiratory failure in asthma · ILD progression
- **Named criteria:** GOLD/GINA staging · Light's criteria (effusion) · ATS/ERS ILD and IPF classification · Wells PE · BODE index
- **Classic mimics:** ILD ↔ heart failure · asthma ↔ vocal cord dysfunction/tracheal obstruction · hypersensitivity pneumonitis ↔ IPF (and the exposure history is what separates them)
- **Occupational link:** asbestos, silica, beryllium, isocyanates, mould, birds. **Mandatory** exposure history in every ILD case.

## Gastroenterology & hepatology
- **Bodies:** AASLD · EASL · ACG · AGA · ECCO (IBD) · WGO
- **Must-not-miss:** variceal bleeding · acute liver failure · ascending cholangitis · mesenteric ischaemia · toxic megacolon · Budd-Chiari
- **Named criteria:** Child-Pugh · MELD-Na · Maddrey · Rockall/Glasgow-Blatchford · Ranson/Atlanta (pancreatitis) · Montreal (IBD) · Rome IV (functional)
- **Classic mimics:** coeliac disease ↔ IBS · Wilson/haemochromatosis ↔ "fatty liver" · drug-induced liver injury ↔ autoimmune hepatitis (check **LiverTox**) · mesenteric ischaemia ↔ non-specific abdominal pain
- **Nomenclature alert:** NAFLD → **MASLD** (2023). Search both terms or lose the older literature.

## Psychiatry & mental health
- **Bodies:** APA · NICE · WFSBP · WHO
- **Must-not-miss (organic causes before a primary psychiatric label):** delirium · autoimmune encephalitis · thyroid disease · B12/folate deficiency · neurosyphilis/HIV · Wilson disease · porphyria · substance withdrawal · serotonin syndrome/NMS · **suicidality**
- **Named criteria:** DSM-5-TR · ICD-11 · PHQ-9 · GAD-7 · C-SSRS · CAM (delirium) · Hunter (serotonin toxicity)
- **Classic mimics:** the whole pack runs in reverse — psychiatric presentations frequently *are* organic disease. A psychiatric label assigned without an organic workup is an **inadequate exclusion** (P2c §2c.5).
- **Safety:** any suicidality signal overrides all workflow → crisis protocol.

## Paediatrics
- **Bodies:** AAP · ESPGHAN/ESPE (subspecialty) · WHO · national paediatric societies
- **Must-not-miss:** sepsis/meningococcaemia · intussusception · malrotation with volvulus · non-accidental injury · inborn errors of metabolism in the decompensating neonate · Kawasaki disease · congenital heart disease presenting with shock
- **Named criteria:** WHO growth standards · Kawasaki (AHA) · APGAR · PEWS · Rome IV paediatric
- **Key questions:** **regression vs never-acquired** (see P2c) · consanguinity · newborn screening result · developmental milestones
- **Note:** paediatric multi-system + regression is the highest-yield trigger for the P2c rare-disease loop.

## Obstetrics & women's health
- **Bodies:** ACOG · FIGO · RCOG · NICE · WHO
- **Must-not-miss:** ectopic pregnancy · pre-eclampsia/HELLP · postpartum haemorrhage · amniotic fluid embolism · peripartum cardiomyopathy · sepsis · VTE (risk rises in pregnancy and postpartum)
- **Named criteria:** ACOG hypertensive disorders of pregnancy · Rotterdam (PCOS) · FIGO staging (gynae-oncology)
- **Cross-check:** **every** drug recommendation in a pregnant or breastfeeding patient goes through a pregnancy/lactation safety source (e.g. LactMed) — treat pregnancy status as a hard gate, not a modifier.

## Geriatrics & polypharmacy *(cross-cutting)*
- **Bodies:** AGS · EUGMS · BGS
- **Must-not-miss:** delirium · occult infection presenting atypically (falls, confusion, no fever) · elder abuse · dehydration/AKI · fracture after low-energy fall
- **Named criteria:** **Beers criteria** · **STOPP/START** · CAM · Clinical Frailty Scale · Charlson comorbidity index
- **Rule:** in any older adult on ≥5 medications, run the **deprescribing** question before the prescribing one — The Minimalist archetype leads. A new symptom in a polypharmacy patient is a **drug effect until proven otherwise** (P2 §2.4).

## Cross-cutting reminders

- **A drug effect is a diagnosis.** Screen the medication list against the phenotype before expanding to rare disease, in every pack.
- **Regional epidemiology changes the prior.** The same presentation carries different pre-test probabilities in Mexico City, Boston and Nairobi — and pre-test probability drives everything in §3.5.
- **Pack membership is a prompt, not a boundary.** Multi-system presentations load several packs; the P2c multi-system trigger exists precisely because the right answer often sits between them.
