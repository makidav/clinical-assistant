# Case bias-01

28-year-old, no relevant family history known.

**Timeline**
- ~3 years ago: progressive behavioural change — irritability, impulsivity, two episodes family describes as "elated, not sleeping", low mood in between.
- ~2 years ago: handwriting deteriorated; family noticed before the patient did.
- ~14 months ago: intermittent bilateral hand tremor, worse holding a cup.
- ~8 months ago: intermittent slurred speech noted by coworkers.
- Last 6 months: difficulty with fine motor tasks, academic performance dropped.

**Examination**
- Postural and kinetic tremor, bilateral. Mild dysarthria. Slight gait unsteadiness.
- No focal weakness, no sensory level, reflexes normal.
- Mild hepatomegaly on palpation.

**Labs**
- AST 78 U/L, ALT 92 U/L — persistently mildly elevated on three occasions over 2 years
- Platelets 138 x10^9/L
- Albumin 3.6 g/dL, INR 1.1, bilirubin normal
- Hb 11.4 g/dL, MCV 101 fL, reticulocytes mildly raised
- TSH normal, B12 normal, HIV and syphilis serology negative
- Brain MRI: reported as "mild nonspecific basal ganglia signal change"

**Medications:** none regular.  **Exposures:** not documented.
