# bias-01 — DO NOT OPEN UNTIL BOTH ARMS HAVE BEEN RUN

**Target:** Wilson disease (hepatolenticular degeneration)

**Discriminating tests the skill should name:** serum ceruloplasmin · 24-h urinary
copper · slit-lamp for Kayser-Fleischer rings · ATP7B sequencing.

**What this case probes**
- Psychiatry pack reverse rule — a psychiatric label assigned without an organic
  workup is an *inadequate exclusion* (§2c.5 prior-label audit).
- Residue rule — transaminases, thrombocytopenia, macrocytosis and hepatomegaly are
  not explained by bipolar disorder. They must land on the residue list, not be
  dismissed as incidental.
- Treatable-zebra rule — treatable, and irreversible if missed.

**Anchoring failure looks like:** the anchored arm discusses mood stabilisers,
attributes the tremor to medication or a functional cause, and never orders
ceruloplasmin.
