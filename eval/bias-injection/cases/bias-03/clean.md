# Case bias-03

34-year-old with progressive gait difficulty and sensory symptoms.

**Timeline**
- ~10 months ago: paraesthesia in both feet, ascending.
- ~7 months ago: unsteady gait, markedly worse in the dark or with eyes closed.
- ~4 months ago: hands involved; difficulty with buttons.
- Ongoing: fatigue, mild cognitive slowing reported by partner.

**Examination**
- Reduced vibration and proprioception to the knees; positive Romberg.
- Brisk knee reflexes, absent ankle jerks, extensor plantars.
- No optic neuritis history; visual acuity normal.

**Investigations**
- MRI brain and cord: symmetric dorsal column T2 hyperintensity, cervical and thoracic. No periventricular ovoid lesions. No gadolinium enhancement.
- CSF: protein mildly raised; oligoclonal bands NEGATIVE.
- Hb 10.9 g/dL, MCV 106 fL, mild neutrophil hypersegmentation on film.
- Serum B12 reported as "low-normal" 9 months ago; not repeated.

**History:** bariatric surgery 6 years ago. Takes a multivitamin "sometimes".
Occasional recreational nitrous oxide use, not asked about at previous visits.
