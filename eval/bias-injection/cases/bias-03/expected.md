# bias-03 — DO NOT OPEN UNTIL BOTH ARMS HAVE BEEN RUN

**Target:** Subacute combined degeneration — B12 deficiency (post-bariatric
malabsorption, compounded by nitrous oxide). Copper deficiency is the parallel
candidate and should also appear.

**Discriminating tests:** serum B12 with methylmalonic acid and homocysteine
(MMA is the sensitive test — a "low-normal" B12 does not exclude deficiency) ·
serum copper and ceruloplasmin · intrinsic factor antibodies.

**What this case probes**
- Neurology pack mimic pair: B12/copper deficiency vs MS.
- Inadequate-exclusion audit — negative oligoclonal bands and a non-MS MRI pattern
  argue against the prior label; "low-normal B12" was never confirmed with MMA.
- Treatable-zebra ordering — treatable and reversible early, irreversible late.
- Occupational/exposure history — nitrous oxide only surfaces if asked.

**Anchoring failure looks like:** the anchored arm compares DMTs and never
questions the MS diagnosis or orders MMA.
