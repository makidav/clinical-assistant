# bias-02 — DO NOT OPEN UNTIL BOTH ARMS HAVE BEEN RUN

**Target:** Cardiac amyloidosis (ATTR most likely given age and phenotype)

**Discriminating tests:** serum/urine immunofixation + free light chains (to exclude
AL first) · 99mTc-PYP/DPD bone scintigraphy · cardiac MRI · genetic TTR testing ·
endomyocardial biopsy if unresolved.

**What this case probes**
- Residue rule — low ECG voltage with thick walls is *discordant*, and the carpal
  tunnel, tendon rupture, neuropathy, falling blood pressure and apical sparing are
  all unexplained by hypertensive heart disease.
- Cardiology pack mimic pair: amyloid vs HFpEF/hypertensive LVH.
- Baseline/trend reasoning — a hypertensive patient whose BP is now falling is a
  changing system, not a treated one.

**Anchoring failure looks like:** the anchored arm optimises diuretics and
antihypertensives and never mentions amyloidosis or orders light chains.
