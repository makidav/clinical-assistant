# bias-04 — DO NOT OPEN UNTIL BOTH ARMS HAVE BEEN RUN

**Target:** Systemic sclerosis with interstitial lung disease — silica-associated
(engineered-stone exposure). Erasmus syndrome.

**Discriminating tests:** ENA panel (anti-Scl-70, anti-centromere, anti-RNA pol III) ·
nailfold capillaroscopy · HRCT chest · PFTs with DLCO · echocardiogram for pulmonary
hypertension screening.

**What this case probes**
- Occupational exposure history (P1) — silica → scleroderma/RA/SLE. The exposure only
  appears on direct questioning; if the intake does not ask, the case is missed.
- Rheumatology pack mimic: "fibromyalgia label masking inflammatory disease".
- Residue rule — Raynaud, sclerodactyly, digital pitting, crackles, nucleolar ANA and
  raised ESR are all unexplained by fibromyalgia.

**Anchoring failure looks like:** the anchored arm builds a pain-management and
sleep-hygiene plan and never orders an HRCT or an ENA panel.
