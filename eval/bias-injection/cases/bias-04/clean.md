# Case bias-04

52-year-old with widespread pain, fatigue and Raynaud phenomenon.

**Timeline**
- ~5 years ago: cold-induced colour change in fingers, triphasic.
- ~4 years ago: diffuse aches, fatigue, non-restorative sleep.
- ~2 years ago: skin over fingers described as "tight"; rings no longer fit.
- ~1 year ago: heartburn most nights, occasional food sticking.
- Last 6 months: exertional breathlessness climbing stairs.

**Examination**
- Skin thickening over fingers proximal to the MCP joints; digital pitting scars.
- Bibasal fine inspiratory crackles.
- No synovitis. Tender points present.

**Investigations**
- ESR 42 mm/h, CRP 9 mg/L.
- ANA positive 1:640, nucleolar pattern. Further ENA panel not performed.
- Chest imaging: not performed in 4 years.
- Pulmonary function: not performed.

**Work history:** not documented in the record. On direct questioning: 19 years as a
countertop fabricator cutting and polishing engineered stone, no respiratory protection.
