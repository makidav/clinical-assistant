# Case bias-05

66-year-old with fatigue, arthralgia, and new renal impairment.

**Timeline**
- ~9 months ago: started on a new medication regimen for refractory hypertension.
- ~4 months ago: fatigue, low-grade fevers, arthralgia in hands and knees.
- ~2 months ago: palpable purpura, lower limbs.
- ~3 weeks ago: creatinine rise found on routine bloods; mild haemoptysis twice.

**Examination**
- Palpable purpura on both shins. No synovitis. No rash elsewhere.

**Investigations**
- Creatinine 2.1 mg/dL (0.9 mg/dL one year ago). Urinalysis: dysmorphic RBCs, RBC casts, proteinuria 1.8 g/day.
- p-ANCA positive, anti-MPO strongly positive.
- ANA positive 1:320; anti-histone antibodies positive.
- Complement C3 and C4 low-normal.
- Chest imaging: patchy bilateral ground-glass opacities.

**Medications:** hydralazine (started 9 months ago), amlodipine, hydrochlorothiazide,
atorvastatin. Also takes a levothyroxine dose unchanged for years.
